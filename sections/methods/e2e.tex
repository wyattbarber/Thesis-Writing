\subsection{Device Demonstration}

To demonstrate that the completed system functions as intended, an end-to-end test of the perching process was performed.
The device was attached to the top of a drone airframe, which was then suspended from ropes beneath a target perch
(this setup was used due to restrictions on the operation of drones in research projects). The drone was manually raised from
its starting pose beneath the perch, to a point where the perch was positioned within the grasping mechanism. The expectation
was that at this point, if the system functions correctly, the grasper will close around the perch within the delay of one 
processing cycle of the perch detection. A video was recorded of this process, and still images depicting key stages
of the demonstration are shown in Fig. \ref{fig:e2e-vid}.

One mechanical alteration had to be made to the device for this test to be performed. As mentioned in Section \ref{sec:methods:collection-single},
small distances, less than a 2 to 3 hundred mm, cannot be detected by the device as originally designed due to the large stereo camera baseline.
For this test a new camera mount with a narrower baseline was fabricated and new calibration data for this mount was collected following
the procedure in Section \ref{sec:methods:calibration}. Changing the baseline changes the resolution of the stereo cameras depth measurement,
and the rate at which resolution changes vs distance, but it does not change the mechanism of detecting perches once a calibrated point cloud is 
produces. Therefore, the results in Sections \ref{sec:results:perch-rate} and \ref{sec:results:perch-accuracy} are still valid. 

To verify that the perch detection performs comparably at small distances, the procedure in Section \ref{sec:methods:collection-single}
was replicated with the test object placed at a distance of 40mm, 80mm, and 160mm. The success rate and accuracy of detection at these distances
was determined as described in Sections \ref{sec:methods:analysis-success-rate} and \ref{sec:methods:analysis-accuracy}, and the results 
are shown in Sections \ref{sec:results:perch-rate} and \ref{sec:results:perch-accuracy}.
