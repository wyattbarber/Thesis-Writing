\subsection{Device Demonstration}

The demonstration was successful, with the device successfully triggering the grasping mechanism to close 
after a perch came into range. The delay between the perch coming into grasping range and the 
grasper being closed was approximately 10 seconds, based on timestamps of the corresponding video frames.
Images of the drones position at key stages in the process are show in 
Fig. \ref{fig:e2e-vid}.
