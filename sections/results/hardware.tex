\subsection{Verification of Grasper}

\subsubsection{Holding Capacity} \label{sec:results:capacity}

During the testing of the device's holding capacity, the maximum test weight 
was reached without reaching the point of failure. The final total weight
supported by the device was 8.83 kg.
After accounting for the electronics that were removed for the test, the minimum verified 
holding capacity of the device is 8.76 kg. 

\subsubsection{Weight and Repeatability} \label{sec:results:weight-repeatability}

Table \ref{tab:weights} shows the weights of individual components of the tested device, 
as well as the total weight. Images of the individual components are included in table \ref{tab:weights}
for clarity.

In the repeatability test, all 100 of the test cycles resulted in successful grasping and releasing. 
No degradation or other change in the behavior (such as slowed motion, jitter, or other unintended behavior)
was observed.
