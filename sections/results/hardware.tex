\subsection{Verification of Grasper}

\subsubsection{Holding Capacity} \label{sec:results:capacity}

During the testing of the devices holding capacity, all available weights 
were added without reaching the point of failure. The final total weight
supported by the device was 8.83 kg.
After accounting for the electronics that were removed for the test, the verified 
holding capacity of the device is 8.76 kg. The actual limit is still
unknown, due to the limited number of test weights available.
